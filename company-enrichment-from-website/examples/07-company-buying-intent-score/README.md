# Score companies by buying intent: who to contact first and why

Turn on Pro mode to get, for every company, a buying-intent score from 0 to 100 with the public signals behind it (hiring and open roles, recent posts, sales and marketing tools), an estimated company size, a fit score against your ideal customer and a 'why now' line for your outreach. Sort by pro.buyingIntentScore and call the top of the list first.

- Actor: [Company Enrichment from Website](https://apify.com/cleanyield/company-enrichment-from-website)
- Real run: 2 results in 3.5 s (refreshed 2026-11)
- `input.json`: paste it in the Actor input (JSON tab) or send it to the API.
- `output-sample.json`: the first 5 results of that run.

**Limitations:** Scores use only what the company's own website shows (no third-party databases): a company that hides its job board or tools scores lower. The one-sentence AI insight needs the AI to be available; when it is not, 'insight' is null and is not charged.
