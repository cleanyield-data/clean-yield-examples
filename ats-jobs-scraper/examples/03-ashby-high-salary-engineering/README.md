# Ashby jobs with salary: engineering roles paying $200k+

Find engineering jobs paying at least USD 200,000 a year at OpenAI, Ramp, Notion and Linear (all on Ashby). Ashby's public API publishes compensation tiers; the Actor turns them into salaryMin/Max, currency, period and annualized values, then filters. 358 jobs matched.

- Actor: [ATS Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/cleanyield/ats-jobs-scraper)
- Real run: 356 results in 6.4 s (refreshed 2026-11-05)
- `input.json`: paste it in the Actor input (JSON tab) or send it to the API.
- `output-sample.json`: the first 5 results of that run.

**Limitations:** The salary filter compares in the currency you choose (no exchange-rate conversion). Jobs without a published salary are excluded unless includeJobsWithoutSalary is on.
