# Hiring signals for sales: which companies are hiring engineers right now (Pro mode)

Pro mode on two companies: every job comes with the company hiring score (0–100), whether hiring is accelerating, jobs posted in the last 30 days, a factual "why now" line for outreach, the technologies named in the job and an AI insight sentence (when the AI key is configured). Run it on a schedule and companyTrend shows open jobs then vs now since your first run.

- Actor: [ATS Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/cleanyield/ats-jobs-scraper)
- Real run: 4 results in 5.2 s (refreshed 2026-11)
- `input.json`: paste it in the Actor input (JSON tab) or send it to the API.
- `output-sample.json`: the first 5 results of that run.

**Limitations:** The AI insight sentence needs the AI to be configured; without it the field is null and the rest of Pro mode is delivered. companyTrend appears from the second run on (history starts with your first Pro run). Pro mode adds a per-job fee on top of the job price.
