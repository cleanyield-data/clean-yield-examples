# Remote jobs in the US at remote-first startups

Watchlist of remote-friendly companies (GitLab, Discord, PostHog, Supabase, JumpCloud, ElevenLabs) mixing Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby. Filters: remote only, United States or remote locations, posted in the last 30 days. 240 jobs matched.

- Actor: [ATS Jobs Scraper](https://apify.com/cleanyield/ats-jobs-scraper)
- Real run: 241 results in 6.4 s (refreshed 2026-11-05)
- `input.json`: paste it in the Actor input (JSON tab) or send it to the API.
- `output-sample.json`: the first 5 results of that run.

**Limitations:** When an ATS does not state the workplace type, it is deduced from the location text ("Remote (US)"); jobs with no signal at all are excluded by the remote filter. The 21 s include probing six bare names on three ATS; pasting board URLs is faster.
