# Who wins IT government tenders in Colombia (awardees and amounts)

Awarded IT and software processes from SECOP II in the last 60 days with the winner's name and NIT and the awarded amount: competitive intelligence for suppliers who want to know who wins in their sector and at what price.

- Actor: [Latin America Public Tenders](https://apify.com/cleanyield/latam-public-tenders)
- Real run: 10 results in 2.8 s (refreshed 2026-11)
- `input.json`: paste it in the Actor input (JSON tab) or send it to the API.
- `output-sample.json`: the first 5 results of that run.

**Limitations:** Awardees who are individuals (sole traders) are hidden by default (awardeeData: omitIndividuals); companies are shown. The NIT/person distinction in Colombia is a heuristic.
