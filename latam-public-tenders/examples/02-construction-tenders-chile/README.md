# Public works and construction tenders in Chile over USD 100,000

Open construction and public-works tenders (licitaciones de obras) published on Mercado Público (ChileCompra) in the last 3 days with an estimated budget above USD 100,000, converted from CLP with today's exchange rate. Deadlines are given both in Chilean local time and in correct UTC.

- Actor: [Latin America Public Tenders](https://apify.com/cleanyield/latam-public-tenders)
- Real run: 10 results in 193.7 s (refreshed 2026-11)
- `input.json`: paste it in the Actor input (JSON tab) or send it to the API.
- `output-sample.json`: the first 5 results of that run.

**Limitations:** About half of Chilean tenders don't publish an estimated amount: with minAmountUsd they are excluded. Chile needs one detail request per tender, so filtered searches take minutes (194 s here).
